Stompbox Lab

Stompbox Lab is a pedal board and keyboard synth for anyone who wants to play and layer sounds across several phones.

You stomp pedals on, turn knobs by dragging, and play a keyboard synth. A loop player runs grooves with a tempo and parts for drums, bass, lead, and pad. Signing in saves your board, and one room can hold every phone in the band.

Live at stompbox-lab.vercel.app
